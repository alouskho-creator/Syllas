Syllas – Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

Syllas (“we,” “our,” or “us”) operates the Syllas AI study and chat application and related services (the “Service”).

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard information when you use our app and website.

By using Syllas, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

⸻

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

a. Account Information

When you create or access an account, we may collect:
	•	Email address
	•	Display name or username
	•	Authentication information (such as Google Sign-In or Apple Sign-In data)

Authentication is securely handled using third-party providers such as Firebase Authentication (Google).

⸻

b. User-Provided Content

When using AI-powered features, you may voluntarily submit:
	•	Chat messages
	•	Prompts and questions
	•	Notes and study content
	•	Uploaded images or files

This content may contain personal information if you choose to provide it.

⸻

c. Usage and Technical Data

We may collect limited technical data such as:
	•	Device type
	•	Operating system
	•	App version
	•	Interaction and feature usage data

This information is used to improve reliability, performance, and security.

⸻

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:
	•	Provide and operate Syllas features
	•	Authenticate users and manage accounts
	•	Provide AI-powered responses and study tools
	•	Maintain subscription functionality (e.g., Pro features)
	•	Improve app performance and reliability
	•	Prevent abuse and maintain security
	•	Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell personal data.

⸻

3. AI Processing and Third-Party Data Sharing

Syllas provides AI-powered features such as chat responses, quizzes, study guides, and image analysis.

To provide these features:
	•	User-provided content (such as messages, prompts, images, or files) may be transmitted to third-party AI providers (such as OpenAI) for processing.
	•	This data is used solely to generate responses and provide requested functionality.

Explicit User Consent

Before any user content is transmitted to a third-party AI service:
	•	The app displays an AI Data Usage Notice
	•	Users must tap “Agree & Continue” to provide explicit consent
	•	If the user declines, no data is transmitted and AI features remain disabled

AI Training

Syllas does not use user content to train general-purpose AI models.

Third-Party Protections

Third-party providers used by Syllas (including OpenAI and Firebase) are contractually required to implement security measures and data protections equal to or greater than industry standards.

⸻

4. Third-Party Services

Syllas relies on trusted third-party providers, including:
	•	OpenAI – AI processing
	•	Firebase (Google) – authentication, database, hosting
	•	Subscription and payment providers
	•	Analytics and monitoring services

These services receive only the information necessary to perform their functions and are required to protect user data.

⸻

5. Data Storage and Security

We use industry-standard encryption and secure APIs to protect user data.

All network communication uses HTTPS encryption provided by the operating system.

While we implement strong safeguards, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

⸻

6. Data Retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary to:
	•	Provide our services
	•	Maintain user accounts
	•	Comply with legal obligations

Users may request deletion of their account and associated data at any time through the app settings or by contacting us.

Upon deletion, data is removed or anonymized in accordance with our retention practices.
